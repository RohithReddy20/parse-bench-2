## What `respell` does

A word the reading spells slightly differently from the text layer takes the layer's spelling. The reading's
tokens (markup and link targets masked) are aligned to the layer's in reading order; only one-for-one swaps of
up to two words, with two matching words on each side, are considered, and only small ones: one edit in a word of
four letters or more, a quarter of the letters in a long word, one digit in a number of the same length. It fixes
`gamers` → `garners`, `jhunewslatter` → `jhunewsletter` in a URL, Turkish dotless i, Russian and Greek letters the
model normalised, `blore` → `bhore`.

The layer must be trustworthy on the page, which is where the first prototype failed (dev −0.11, check −1.03):

- **Visible text only.** An OCR layer is not the page: text drawn in the invisible render mode, or text covered by
  an image drawn over the whole page afterwards (a scan laid over its own OCR layer), is refused. Those layers are
  older, weaker OCR than OCRia-1 and caused most of the losses.
- **Glyphs that map to unicode.** Pages where over 1% of the characters have a unicode mapping error are refused.
- **The layer holds the reading's words** (80% of its tokens occur in the reading).
- **No layer artefacts.** A swap is refused when the layer's word has a modifier letter, a capital inside a
  lower-case word (`speciMcally`, `oAering`: mis-mapped ligature glyphs), or when the edit falls on a ligature
  (`fi`, `fl`, `ff`, `ft`) of the read word (`nancial`, `fotograieren`).

With those guards the prototype touched 32 pages: 26 up, 1 down (a 0.01 "filing" → "filling").

The brief's warning holds: words the read lacks are mostly not omissions. With a moved-block filter almost no
insertion candidates are left, so "omitted" (1.50) cannot be recovered from the layer by position.

Where the rest sits, by text-layer coverage of the reference (analysis script): pages whose layer covers 80% of
the reference lose 4.6 points, pages with a partial or broken layer (Indic scripts, mixed OCR) 4.1, pages with no
layer 3.2. On the healthy-layer pages the losses are mostly structural (blocks the model left out or added, reading
order), which the layer cannot place reliably; on the other two groups the layer cannot help at all. The best next
idea for text is a second OCRia-1 read (another resolution or a crop) on the pages whose read disagrees most with
a trustworthy layer, which needs the GPU pass.
